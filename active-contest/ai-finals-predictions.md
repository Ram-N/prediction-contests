## Claude

| Match | Winner | Confidence |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Semifinal 1 | South Africa | 78% |
| Semifinal 2 | India | 62% |
| Final | **South Africa** | 55% |

**Reasoning**

**Key factors influencing my decisions:**

1. **South Africa's unbeaten run.** The Proteas are the only undefeated team and have won all but one of their matches comprehensively. They have dominant batting depth (Markram with 268 runs) and bowling firepower (Ngidi with 12 wickets). They already beat New Zealand by seven wickets during the group stage.

2. **New Zealand's inconsistency.** The Black Caps have lost two of their six completed matches and weren't entirely convincing, qualifying largely on net run rate.

3. **India's home advantage and form.** India are defending champions playing in Mumbai, a ground they know intimately. India have won nine of the last 12 T20Is against England, including five of the past six encounters. Samson is in career-best form.

4. **England's clinical campaign.** England topped Super 8 Group 2 after winning all three matches, with Adil Rashid (16 wickets) and Harry Brook (284 runs) in superb touch. They are a genuine threat.

5. **South Africa's 76-run demolition of India** in the Super 8s is a psychological edge that could prove decisive if they meet in the final.

**Alignment with betting markets:** India remain outright favorites in the market. My picks partially diverge — I agree SA should beat NZ (market consensus), and I agree India should edge England (slight favorite). However, I'm picking South Africa as champion over India, which goes against the market's top pick. I believe the market is overweighting India's home advantage and underweighting SA's comprehensive superiority across all phases of this tournament, including their head-to-head thrashing of India.

**Downside risks:**
- **SF1 (SA vs NZ):** Eden Gardens' dew factor could neutralize SA's bowling if they bat first, and NZ's Mitchell Santner is experienced in Indian conditions. Knockout cricket is NZ's specialty.
- **SF2 (India vs England):** England dismantled India 10-0 in the 2022 semifinal in similar circumstances. Rashid's 16 wickets could be the difference if India's middle order wobbles again.
- **Final (SA champion):** South Africa carry the weight of ICC tournament history — perennial chokers in finals. The 2024 final loss from a winning position is a genuine mental scar, and India at the Narendra Modi Stadium with 130,000 fans is an intimidating prospect.
