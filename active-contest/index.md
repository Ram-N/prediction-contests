# FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Contest

Welcome to the FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Contest! This year we're running **two separate contests** — enter one or both.

## Deadline: June 11, before the first kickoff. Don't wait!

---

## Contest 1: Group Stage Predictions

Pick the **2 teams from each group** that will advance to the Round of 32, plus **8 best third-place teams**.

**Scoring:** 1 point for each correct pick (max 32 points). Highest score wins.

## Contest 2: 4-2-1 Long Range Predictions

Go big! Pick **4 Semifinalists**, **2 Finalists**, and the **World Cup Winner** — all before the tournament starts.

**Scoring:** 1 point per correct semifinalist, 2 per correct finalist, 4 for the correct winner (max 12 points). Highest score wins.
