Here is a clean, structured prompt you can copy and give to each AI model.

---

# PROMPT: ICC T20 World Cup Knockout Prediction Challenge (Research Allowed)

You are participating in a structured sports prediction experiment for the ICC Men’s T20 World Cup knockout stage.

We are at the final phase of the tournament. Only **three matches remain**:

## Semifinal 1

South Africa vs New Zealand

## Semifinal 2

India vs England

## Final

Winner of Semifinal 1 vs Winner of Semifinal 2
Venue: Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad, India

---

## Your Task

You must make **three definitive predictions**:

1. Winner of Semifinal 1
2. Winner of Semifinal 2
3. Winner of the Final (ICC Champion)

No hedging. No multiple scenarios. Make one clear call for each.

---

## Research Policy

You are explicitly allowed to use any available information, including:

* Current team form
* Head-to-head history
* Player availability and injuries
* Venue conditions
* Toss bias and pitch reports
* Statistical models
* Betting markets
* Expert analysis
* News reports
* Any other relevant public data

You may use as many sources as needed.

However, do not blindly copy betting odds. Use structured reasoning and independent judgment.

---

## Scoring Rules (for context)

* Semifinal 1 correct prediction: 4 points
* Semifinal 2 correct prediction: 4 points
* Final winner correct prediction: 4 points

Maximum total: **12 points**

Accuracy matters. Confidence matters.

---

## Required Output Format (Strict)

```
Semifinal 1
Winner: [Team Name]
Confidence: [0–100%]

Semifinal 2
Winner: [Team Name]
Confidence: [0–100%]

Final
Champion: [Team Name]
Confidence: [0–100%]
```

---

## Reasoning Section (max 300 words total)

After the predictions, include a short reasoning section that:

1. Identifies the 3–5 most important factors influencing your decisions.
2. States whether your picks align with betting favorites or differ from them.
3. If aligned, explain why you independently agree.
4. If different, explain why you believe the market may be mispricing risk.
5. Explicitly mention one downside risk for each semifinal and the final prediction.

Do not hedge excessively. Make a clear call.
