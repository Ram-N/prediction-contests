Here are my predictions for your 2026 FIFA World Cup contest.

### 1. Semifinalists

* Spain
* France
* England
* Argentina

### 2. Finalists

* Spain
* France

### 3. World Cup Champion

* Spain

### 4. Brief Justification

Spain enters the tournament with an elite mix of youth and experience. Their midfield is incredible and their control of the ball naturally suffocates opponents in knockout matches.

France is right there with them. They have deep attacking options and elite star power. Kylian Mbappé alone makes them a massive threat to reach the final.

England boasts one of their most talented squads in decades. They are primed for a deep run but might lack the tactical edge to beat the absolute best in a semifinal.

Argentina is the defending champion. They know exactly how to handle the pressure of tournament football and they will ride that experience far.

I predict Spain to take the trophy. They have the most reliable baseline of any contender and they just proved they can win big tournaments at Euro 2024. Their control of the game will push them past France in the final.
