### 5. Reasoning

**Group compositions** come from the finalized post-playoff draw: Group A is Mexico, South Africa, South Korea, Czechia; B is Canada, Bosnia, Qatar, Switzerland; C is Brazil, Morocco, Haiti, Scotland; D is the US, Paraguay, Australia, Türkiye; E is Germany, Curaçao, Ivory Coast, Ecuador; F is Netherlands, Japan, Sweden, Tunisia; G is Belgium, Egypt, Iran, New Zealand; H is Spain, Cape Verde, Saudi Arabia, Uruguay; I is France, Senegal, Iraq, Norway; J is Argentina, Algeria, Austria, Jordan; K is Portugal, DR Congo, Uzbekistan, Colombia; L is England, Croatia, Ghana, Panama.

**Seeds win their groups.** With one Pot 1 side per group and at most one other elite team, I take every seed first except where a host's home advantage applies. The three hosts all top relatively soft groups — even Canada, where Switzerland is close, gets the edge from playing all three matches at home. The key scoring insight: with the top two from each group plus the eight best third-place teams advancing — 32 of 48 teams — the 1st/2nd/3rd ordering within a group mostly doesn't matter for points; what matters is correctly separating the ~32 plausible advancers from the 16 genuinely weak sides.

**Toss-up calls.** Group I order between Senegal and Norway is nearly a coin flip, but both advance regardless (Norway's Haaland-led attack makes them comfortably the strongest projected third-place team). Same logic in Group J with Algeria/Austria — Argentina to win Group J with Algeria second was flagged as a value bet, and Austria still advances as a third-place side. Group E ordering follows the data: Ecuador had the best defensive record in CONMEBOL qualifying, conceding five goals in 18 matches, while Ivory Coast arrives with a single loss since October — and that loss column doesn't even capture their June friendly win over France. Group K: Colombia's veteran core (Luis Díaz, Jhon Arias, Richard Ríos) makes the Portugal–Colombia finale the likely group decider, but both clearly advance over Uzbekistan and DR Congo.

**The third-place cut.** The advancing eight are the third-place teams projected to win at least one match convincingly and draw another (4+ points): Norway, Ivory Coast, Austria, and Sweden could realistically take 6. The eliminated four sit in groups where their only winnable match is against the group's weakest side, projecting to ~3 points with poor goal difference — historically the elimination zone. Bosnia at #9 is my closest call against Scotland at #8; Scotland gets the nod because a draw with Morocco is more plausible than Bosnia taking anything off Switzerland or Canada.

**Biggest risks to this slate:** Mexico's shaky form (Group A may have the most question marks of any group), the Senegal/Norway/Iraq scramble behind France, and whether Egypt or Iran underperform in Group G, which would promote Bosnia or Ghana into the third-place qualifying spots.
