# FIFA World Cup 2026 Group Stage Prediction Challenge

I am running a prediction contest for the FIFA World Cup 2026 Group Stage.

The tournament groups can be found here:

`curl https://worldcup26.ir/get/groups`

You may use any resources available to you, including web searches, betting odds, power rankings, team statistics, expert predictions, or any other relevant information.

## Your Task

### Part 1: Group Predictions

For each of the 12 groups:

* Predict the team that will finish 1st.
* Predict the team that will finish 2nd.
* Predict the team that will finish 3rd.
* Predict the team that will finish 4th.

The teams finishing 1st and 2nd in each group automatically qualify for the Round of 32.

### Part 2: Best Third-Place Teams

After ranking all teams within their groups:

* Evaluate the 12 third-place teams.
* Identify the **8 best third-place teams** that you believe will advance to the Round of 32.
* Identify the **4 third-place teams** that you believe will be eliminated.

## Required Output

Please provide:

1. A table showing your predicted standings for all 12 groups (1st through 4th).
2. A ranked list of all 12 third-place teams.
3. The 8 third-place teams you predict will advance.
4. The 4 third-place teams you predict will be eliminated.
5. A brief explanation of your reasoning, including any major assumptions, statistical models, betting odds, rankings, injuries, or other factors you considered.

## Scoring

The contest scoring is simple:

* 1 point for each team correctly predicted to advance to the Round of 32.
* There are 32 teams that will advance in total.
* Maximum possible score: 32 points.

If you have any questions about the format or rules before making your predictions, please ask.
