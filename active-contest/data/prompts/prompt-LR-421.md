# FIFA World Cup 2026 Long-Range Prediction Contest

I am running a long-range prediction contest for the FIFA World Cup 2026.

The tournament will feature 48 teams. Your task is to predict the eventual semifinalists, finalists, and World Cup champion before the tournament begins.

You may use any resources available to you, including betting odds, power rankings, Elo ratings, statistical models, expert analysis, historical performance, squad strength, injury information, or any other relevant sources.

The participating teams and group assignments can be found here:

`curl https://worldcup26.ir/get/groups`

## Your Task

Make the following predictions:

### Semifinalists

Predict the four teams that will reach the semifinals.

### Finalists

From your four semifinalists, predict the two teams that will advance to the final.

### World Cup Champion

From your two finalists, predict the team that will win the FIFA World Cup 2026.

## Required Output

Please provide:

### 1. Semifinalists

* Team 1
* Team 2
* Team 3
* Team 4

### 2. Finalists

* Team 1
* Team 2

### 3. World Cup Champion

* Team Name

### 4. Brief Justification

Provide a concise explanation of your selections, including the major factors that influenced your predictions.

## Scoring

Points are awarded as follows:

| Prediction                 |   Points |
| -------------------------- | -------: |
| Correct Semifinalist       |  1 point |
| Correct Finalist           | 2 points |
| Correct World Cup Champion | 4 points |

Maximum possible score: **12 points**

* 4 semifinalists × 1 point = 4 points
* 2 finalists × 2 points = 4 points
* 1 champion × 4 points = 4 points

Total = **12 points**

Please ensure your predictions are internally consistent: the champion must be one of your finalists, and each finalist must be one of your semifinalists.

If you have any questions about the rules before making your predictions, please ask.
