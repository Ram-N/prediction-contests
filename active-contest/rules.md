# FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Contest Rules

## Overview

Two contests are running for the FIFA World Cup 2026. You can enter one or both.

**Deadline for both:** June 11, before the first kickoff.

---

## Contest 1: Group Stage Predictions

### What You Pick

- **Top 2 per group:** For each of the 12 groups (A–L), pick the 2 teams that will advance as group winners and runners-up.
- **Best third-place teams:** From the remaining teams (those you didn't pick as top 2), pick exactly 8 that will advance as the best third-place finishers. Maximum 1 third-place pick per group.

### Scoring

- **1 point** for each correct top-2 pick (max 24 points)
- **1 point** for each correct third-place pick (max 8 points)
- **Maximum possible score: 32 points**
- **Highest score wins.**

## Contest 2: 4-2-1 Long Range Predictions

### What You Pick

- **4 Semifinalists:** Pick any 4 teams from the entire tournament that you think will reach the semifinals.
- **2 Finalists:** From your 4 semifinalists, pick 2 that will make the Final.
- **1 Winner:** From your 2 finalists, pick the World Cup Winner.

### Scoring

| Pick | Points per correct pick | Max picks | Max points |
|------|------------------------|-----------|------------|
| Semifinalist | 1 | 4 | 4 |
| Finalist | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| Winner | 4 | 1 | 4 |
| **Total** | | | **12** |

- **Highest score wins.**

## General Rules

- One entry per person per contest (honor system)
- Predictions must be submitted before the first kickoff on June 11
- You are competing for glory and a spot in the [Prediction Hall of Fame](/prediction-contests/hof)
- Winners will be declared after the respective stages are complete

Good luck!
