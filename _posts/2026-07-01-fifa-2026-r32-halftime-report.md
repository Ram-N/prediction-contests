The Round of 32 attracted **66 predictors** — **62 humans**, **3 AI models** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), and the **Wisdom of the Crowd** (WOTC) aggregate. That's 15 new entrants who joined for the knockout rounds, bringing fresh blood (and fresh opinions) into the contest. Including the group stage veterans, the overall contest now has **80 unique participants** across both rounds.

Seven of sixteen Round of 32 matches are in the books, and the leaderboard looks nothing like it did a week ago. **Alex**, who dominated the group stage with a field-best 27 points, has dropped to third place. **Aarush** and **Tees** now share the lead at **36 points** apiece — and they got there by very different paths.

## The Germany Trap

If the group stage had Uruguay, the Round of 32 has **Germany**.

A staggering **94% of participants** (62 out of 66) picked Germany to beat Paraguay. All three AI models picked Germany. The Wisdom of the Crowd picked Germany.

Paraguay won.

Only **four humans** saw it coming:

> **Bakul Kamani, Chayan Chakrabarti, Shaji, and The Kasher**

That's the same hit rate as Daniel picking Cabo Verde in the group stage — a 6% call that most people couldn't even imagine making. For Shaji, Bakul, and The Kasher, this bold pick translated into 12-point R32 scores. Chayan picked Paraguay too, but his broader contrarian strategy (Japan over Brazil, Sweden over France, Ecuador over Mexico) cost him elsewhere — he sits at just 4 R32 points.

---

## Morocco: The Smart Upset

While Germany's loss blindsided nearly everyone, **Morocco over Netherlands** was a different story. About **36% of participants** (24 out of 66) picked Morocco — making it a credible upset that separated the attentive from the conventional.

Morocco came into the R32 off a strong group stage as Group F runners-up, and they had recent knockout pedigree from 2022. The participants who called this one tend to cluster near the top of the R32 standings.

The twelve participants who scored **12 R32 points** (the best possible after 7 matches — 6 out of 7 correct) split into two camps:

**The Morocco Camp** — picked MAR over NED, missed only Germany:
Tees, Ishaan, Ranga Setlur, Goutham Ekollu, Alok, Sanjeev, Shriya Sateesh, Rupender Dahiya, Srini

**The Paraguay Camp** — picked PAR over GER, missed one other match:
Shaji (missed NED), Bakul Kamani (missed RSA), The Kasher (missed CIV)

Nobody got all 7 right.

## The Contrarian Report

**Chayan Chakrabarti** deserves a special mention. He went full contrarian: South Africa, Paraguay, Morocco, Japan, Sweden, Côte d'Ivoire, Ecuador. That's 6 out of 7 underdog picks.

Result? Two hits (Paraguay and Morocco) and five misses. **4 points**. The contrarian strategy is high-risk, high-reward — and this time it was mostly risk. But those two correct calls show there's signal even in the chaos. If Japan or Sweden had pulled upsets, Chayan would be the story of the tournament.
