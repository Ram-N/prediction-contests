## The KO & Finals Stage: India backers rewarded

With 16 points on the table across the three knockout matches, the KO stage was always going to be decisive — and it was. The final standings reshuffled significantly from where things stood after the Super 8.

### How it played out

**Semi-Final 1 (NZ beat SA):** Only about 15% of participants picked New Zealand over South Africa. Those who did collected 4 points when most of the field got zero — a meaningful swing.

**Semi-Final 2 (IND beat ENG):** India were heavy favourites here, and the crowd agreed — the vast majority had India advancing. England's early exit eliminated a chunk of points for the handful who backed them.

**The Final (IND beat NZ):** 66% of participants picked India to lift the trophy. At 8 points, this was the single biggest scoring moment in the contest. Those 45 India-backers got the full haul. The 15 who picked South Africa (who didn't even make the Final) were left watching.

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/img/cricket/t20-2026-ko-score-distribution.png" alt="KO & Finals score distribution" style="width:90%; display:block; margin:1.5em auto;">

The chart tells the story clearly: **37 participants scored 12/16** — they got India right in the Final but missed one semi-final. **11 scored a perfect 16/16** — correctly calling NZ in SF1, India in SF2, and India in the Final. A tight cluster of 9 participants scored zero.

### The Perfect KO predictors — 16/16

These 11 participants called all three knockout results correctly (NZ, IND, IND):

> **Aakarsh Ekollu, Dhanush Ekollu, Gokul, Goutham Ekollu, Mahesh, Ramesh Srinivasan, Rankanathan v s, Shiv Iyer, Subha, vivek, Vivek (Redwood City)**

Not only did they nail the Final — they also correctly picked New Zealand over South Africa in the semi-final, which the majority of participants got wrong. That contrarian call was where the 16-point scorers separated themselves.

## How the Contest Was Won

Aditya was the leader after the group stage (14 pts — the highest anyone scored there), led after the Super 8 (25 pts), and held on through the KO stage to finish first overall. Consistent excellence across all three stages, with no single catastrophic miss.

What's particularly notable: **Ramesh Srinivasan** made the biggest KO-stage leap, going from around T-9 pre-KO all the way up to T-2 final — picking up the maximum 16 KO points. **Shiv Iyer** and **Vivek** both scored 16 in the KO stage too, propelling them into the top 10 from outside it.

## AI vs. Humans: The Final Verdict

<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/img/cricket/t20-2026-ai-vs-humans-stages.png" alt="AI vs humans average score by stage" style="width:90%; display:block; margin:1.5em auto;">

The AI models outperformed humans on average in the Group Stage and Super 8 — and then the KO stage nearly evened things out. The final tally:

| Model | Group Stage | Super 8 | KO & Finals | Total |
|-------|:-----------:|:-------:|:-----------:|------:|
| Perplexity 🤖 | 12 | 9 | 12 | **33** |
| Gemini 🤖 | 11 | 8 | 12 | **31** |
| ChatGPT 🤖 | 12 | 6 | 12 | **30** |
| Claude 🤖 | 12 | 9 | 4 | **25** |
| *Human average* | *10.3* | *7.3* | *9.5* | *27.2* |
| *Human median* | *10* | *7* | *12* | *28* |

**Perplexity** finishes as the top AI at 33, ranking T-9 overall — right alongside human competitors Ranga and Vivek. **Gemini** (31) and **ChatGPT** (30) are also squarely in the human pack. **Claude** had a rough KO stage: picking South Africa to win the Final was the lone divergence from the other AI models, and it cost 8 points.

The AI models collectively beat the human *average* (27.2) but not the human *median* (28) — and no AI came close to the top human scorers. Aditya's 37 beats every AI model by at least 4 points.

### Where humans beat the machines

The Super 8 is where smart humans separated from the AIs. **Keshav Venkatesh** and **Sackett** both scored a perfect 12 in the Super 8 — the only two participants to do so. They correctly predicted South Africa to finish *above* India in Group 1, a call that every AI model got wrong. That single correct contrarian call was worth 6 extra points right there.

In the group stage, the AIs had a structural edge: their predictions were more calibrated to historical team quality, and teams like India, South Africa and England largely delivered. But the Super 8's SA-above-India result punished the AI consensus hard, while rewarding the small group of humans who trusted their reading of the tournament's momentum over statistical priors.

### The numbers

- **55 of 68 human participants** (81%) outperformed Claude's final total of 25
- **22 of 68 participants** (32%) outperformed Perplexity's 33
- **Top 3 humans** all finished 4+ points above the best AI score
- The AI models' average (29.8) beat the human average (27.2) by about 2.6 points — a real but modest edge
- The AI models' **median** was **31** (between Gemini's 31 and ChatGPT's 30), comfortably above the human median of 28
