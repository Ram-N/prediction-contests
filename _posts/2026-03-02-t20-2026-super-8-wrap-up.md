## Super 8 Highlights

### Perfect Group-Stage Pickers 🎯

Two participants nailed all 12 points from the Super 8 group-stage predictions — correctly picking both qualifiers from both groups *and* their finishing order:

**Keshav Venkatesh** and **Sackett** — both scored a flawless **12/12**.

A round of applause for both. That's not easy.

### Against the Crowd: SA ahead of India

Here's the moment of the Super 8. South Africa topped Group 1, finishing *above India*.

Almost no one saw it coming. Of ~65 participants, only **9 predicted South Africa to finish ahead of India**:

> Chayan Chakrabarti, Dodes, Keshav Narasimhan, Keshav Venkatesh, Mukund N., P Amritha, Sackett, V 'CV' C, Vivek

That's roughly **14% of participants** who got the Group 1 order right. The other 86% — including every single AI model — picked India on top. SA proved them all wrong.

If you're on that list: well done. That was a brave call.

🎉 Shoutout to the SA believers: **Chayan Chakrabarti**, **Dodes**, **Keshav Narasimhan**, **Keshav Venkatesh**, **Mukund N.**, **P Amritha**, **Sackett**, **V 'CV' C**, and **Vivek**. Nine out of sixty-five. The crowd said India. You said South Africa. You were right.

## 🤖 AI vs. Humans: A Moment of Humility

All four AI models — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity — confidently predicted India to top Group 1. South Africa's result was a 0-for-4 miss across the board.

**Final AI standings after the Super 8:**

| Model | Total |
|-------|-------|
| Perplexity 🤖 | 21 |
| Claude 🤖 | 21 |
| Gemini 🤖 | 19 |
| ChatGPT 🤖 | 18 |

Perplexity and Claude are tied 6th overall — respectable, but the SA miss cost all four models dearly. Humans with the courage to go against consensus were rewarded.

### Why did the AIs get it wrong?

The AI models had a structural disadvantage: their training data skews heavily toward historical performance, and India's recent record coming into this tournament was dominant. Every model locked in on India as the near-certain Group 1 leader and found it hard to escape that prior. South Africa's rise — built on form in this specific tournament cycle rather than a long track record of T20 dominance — was exactly the kind of recent, tournament-specific signal that AI models tend to underweight. Humans who watched the games closely, or simply had a gut feel for SA's momentum, had an edge that no amount of historical data could compensate for.
