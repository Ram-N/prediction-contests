Three matches remain. The quarterfinals are done — France, Spain, England, and Argentina all advanced, validating nearly everyone who submitted ST-421 picks. Now the field narrows to the last four.

We have two sources of prediction data to look back on: **LR-421**, submitted in early June before the Round of 32 (55 participants, 51 humans), and **ST-421**, locked in last week before the quarterfinals (62 participants, 59 humans). Let's see what the crowd thought then — and what they think now.

## The collision no one planned for

The biggest story in the data isn't who people picked. It's what the bracket did to those picks.

In early June, participants in LR-421 were asked to predict the two finalists without knowing how the bracket would unfold. The crowd's answer was near-unanimous: **Spain and France** were the two most popular finalist choices.

![LR-421 France-Spain collision]({{ site.baseurl }}/active-contest/data/plots/finals/finals_fra_esp_collision.png){: .img-fluid }

**27% of LR-421 participants — 14 out of 51 humans — predicted a France vs Spain Final.** Another 31% picked Spain without France. Another 25% picked France without Spain.

That France-Spain Final is now mathematically impossible. They ended up in the same semifinal bracket. One of them goes home without a trophy.

The entire LR-421 field essentially predicted this half of the bracket would produce both Finalists. What they didn't predict is that France and Spain would have to fight each other just to get there.

## How winner opinion shifted from June to July

![Winner comparison: LR-421 vs ST-421]({{ site.baseurl }}/active-contest/data/plots/finals/finals_winner_comparison.png){: .img-fluid }

The shift is striking:

**Spain** went from **45% in LR-421** (23 of 51 humans — the runaway favourite) to **just 8% in ST-421** (5 of 59). That's a collapse. Spain is still in the tournament, but the bracket revealed that they'd have to beat France to reach the Final — and by mid-July, most people stopped believing they could.

**France** went the other direction: from **18% in LR-421** to **61% in ST-421**. France are now the overwhelming consensus pick to lift the trophy.

**Brazil** was the second-most-picked winner in early June at 20% — 10 people believed. Brazil didn't make it past the R32. Every one of those 10 predictions is now hatched out.

**Argentina** barely registered in LR-421 (2 picks, 4%) but climbed to 10% in ST-421. They've earned more respect as the tournament progressed.
