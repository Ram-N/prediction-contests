## The Tough Picks That Paid Off

### Paraguay over Germany (6% pick rate)

This was the biggest upset of the Round of 32. A staggering **94% of the field** picked Germany — including all three AI models and the Wisdom of the Crowd. Only **three humans** had the courage (or audacity) to pick Paraguay:

> **Shaji, Bakul Kamani, and The Kasher**

(Chayan Chakrabarti also picked Paraguay, but his broader contrarian strategy cost him elsewhere.)

Shaji's pick is particularly impressive because he *also* got 14 of the other 15 matches right, finishing at 28 points. That's the kind of conviction-plus-consistency that wins contests.

### Morocco over Netherlands (37% pick rate)

Morocco's win over the Netherlands was the "smart upset" — the one that separated those who paid attention to form from those who relied on reputation. About a third of the field called it, but combined with getting the rest right, it was a key differentiator.

**Ranga Setlur** had Morocco AND got everything else except Germany. That combination is what put him two points clear of the pack.

### Egypt over Australia (Match 88)

This was the sleeper pick of the second half. A significant chunk of the field picked Australia, and it cost them. Among the 28-point scorers, **Sanjeev** and **Shaji** both missed this one — it's what separates them from Ranga's 30.
