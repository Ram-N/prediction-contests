The predictions are locked in. **54 participants** — 51 humans and 3 AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini) — have submitted their Group Stage picks for the FIFA World Cup 2026. Before any matches are played, let's take a look at what the crowd thinks.

Each participant picked **2 teams per group** to advance to the Round of 32, plus **8 best third-place teams** that will also qualify.

---

## The Unanimous Four

Four teams were picked by every single predictor to finish in the top 2 of their group:

- **Brazil** (Group C) — 51/51
- **Spain** (Group H) — 51/51
- **France** (Group I) — 51/51
- **Portugal** (Group K) — 51/51

Not a single person — human or AI — thinks any of these teams will miss the top two. That's conviction.

## The Near-Locks

Five more teams were picked by 50 of 51 predictors: **Germany**, **Belgium**, **Argentina**, **Colombia**, and **England**. Add **Netherlands** and **Uruguay** at 49, and **Mexico** and **Switzerland** at 48, and you have 13 teams that virtually everyone agrees will advance from the group stage.

## The Ignored

On the other end of the spectrum, **Haiti** is the only team that not a single predictor picked — not as a top-2 finisher, and not even as a third-place qualifier. Zero votes out of 51. A complete shutout.

**Curaçao** and **Jordan** each received just 2 third-place picks and zero top-2 picks. **Iraq** and **Cabo Verde** got only 1 top-2 pick each.

## The Most Controversial Group: Group D

Most groups have a clear consensus. Not Group D.

The USA is in — 47 of 51 agree on that. But who joins them? That's where it gets interesting:

| Combo | Picks |
|-------|-------|
| USA + Türkiye | 23 |
| USA + Paraguay | 16 |
| USA + Australia | 8 |
| Paraguay + Türkiye | 3 |
| Australia + Paraguay | 1 |

Five different combinations were picked. No single combo even gets to 50%. Compare that to **Group K** (Portugal + Colombia picked by 50 of 51) or **Group H** (Spain + Uruguay picked by 49 of 51) and you see how divided opinion is.

**Group I** (France, Senegal, Norway, Iraq) is another split decision — France + Senegal edges France + Norway by just 26 to 24.
