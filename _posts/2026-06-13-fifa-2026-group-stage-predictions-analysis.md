The predictions are locked in. **54 participants** — 51 humans and 3 AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini) — have submitted their Group Stage picks for the FIFA World Cup 2026. Before any matches are played, let's take a look at what the crowd thinks.

Each participant picked **2 teams per group** to advance to the Round of 32, plus **8 best third-place teams** that will also qualify.

---

## The Unanimous Four

Four teams were picked by every single predictor to finish in the top 2 of their group:

- **Brazil** (Group C) — 51/51
- **Spain** (Group H) — 51/51
- **France** (Group I) — 51/51
- **Portugal** (Group K) — 51/51

Not a single person — human or AI — thinks any of these teams will miss the top two. That's conviction.

## The Near-Locks

Five more teams were picked by 50 of 51 predictors: **Germany**, **Belgium**, **Argentina**, **Colombia**, and **England**. Add **Netherlands** and **Uruguay** at 49, and **Mexico** and **Switzerland** at 48, and you have 13 teams that virtually everyone agrees will advance from the group stage.

## The Ignored

On the other end of the spectrum, **Haiti** is the only team that not a single predictor picked — not as a top-2 finisher, and not even as a third-place qualifier. Zero votes out of 51. A complete shutout.

**Curaçao** and **Jordan** each received just 2 third-place picks and zero top-2 picks. **Iraq** and **Cabo Verde** got only 1 top-2 pick each.

## The Most Controversial Group: Group D

Most groups have a clear consensus. Not Group D.

The USA is in — 47 of 51 agree on that. But who joins them? That's where it gets interesting:

| Combo | Picks |
|-------|-------|
| USA + Türkiye | 23 |
| USA + Paraguay | 16 |
| USA + Australia | 8 |
| Paraguay + Türkiye | 3 |
| Australia + Paraguay | 1 |

Five different combinations were picked. No single combo even gets to 50%. Compare that to **Group K** (Portugal + Colombia picked by 50 of 51) or **Group H** (Spain + Uruguay picked by 49 of 51) and you see how divided opinion is.

## The Third-Place Race

With 48 teams and only 32 advancing, the 8 best third-place slots are crucial.

![The Third Place Race]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/soccer/third_place_race.png){: .img-fluid }

Ghana and Scotland stand out — over half the field expects them to be among the best third-place finishers. Sweden and Australia are close behind. These are teams that predictors respect enough to advance, just not quite enough to crack the top 2 in their groups.

## The AI Consensus

Three AI models — ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — submitted picks alongside our 51 human predictors. How much do they agree with each other?

**All three agree on 8 of 12 groups**: B (Canada + Switzerland), C (Brazil + Morocco), D (USA + Türkiye), E (Germany + Ecuador), F (Netherlands + Japan), H (Spain + Uruguay), K (Portugal + Colombia), and L (England + Croatia). That's a lot of consensus from three different models.

Where they disagree is more interesting:

| Group | ChatGPT | Claude | Gemini |
|-------|---------|--------|--------|
| A | Mexico + **Korea Republic** | Mexico + **Korea Republic** | Mexico + **Czechia** |
| G | Belgium + **Iran** | Belgium + **Egypt** | Belgium + **Egypt** |
| I | France + **Norway** | France + **Senegal** | France + **Senegal** |
| J | Argentina + **Austria** | Argentina + **Algeria** | Argentina + **Austria** |

Gemini is the lone AI betting on Czechia over Korea Republic in Group A. ChatGPT stands alone picking Iran (Group G) and Norway (Group I) — in both cases, Claude and Gemini side with Egypt and Senegal instead. And Claude is the only model backing Algeria over Austria in Group J.

In other words, ChatGPT is the contrarian of the three, disagreeing with the other two in 3 out of 4 split groups.
