## The AI Bloc: Unanimous on Everything. Almost.

For the first time in this contest, all three AI models submitted **identical quarterfinal picks**: France, Spain, England, Argentina. No divergence across four matches — a clean sweep of consensus picks.

The SF1 pick is also unanimous across all three: France.

SF2: all three picked Argentina.

Then it falls apart on the winner.

All three AIs predict a France vs Argentina final — they just can't agree on who lifts the trophy.

## Match by Match: From the Settled to the Knife-Edge

### ARG v SUI — The Most Unanimous Pick (97%)

**60 of 62 participants picked Argentina.** The two holdouts are Chayan Chakrabarti and Joydeep Dey. That's it. Of all 20+ picks across four rounds of this contest, this is the single most lopsided prediction the field has ever made.

![ARG v SUI prediction split]({{ site.baseurl }}/active-contest/data/plots/st421/st421_ARG_v_SUI.png){: .img-fluid }

### FRA v MAR — Already Decided (87% called it)

54 of 62 picked France. France won. The 8 who picked Morocco — Alok, Ashish Naik, Chayan, Kunal Soni, MK, Nitin Khanna, Sahana, and Shaji — are eliminated from QF1 points.

![FRA v MAR prediction split]({{ site.baseurl }}/active-contest/data/plots/st421/st421_FRA_v_MAR.png){: .img-fluid }

### ESP v BEL — Comfortable Favourite (84% Spain)

52 back Spain, 10 back Belgium. The Belgium contingent is a more eclectic group than the Morocco one: it includes **Prasad R** (who just won the R16), Daniel, GAUTAM, Mukund N., and Aravind Sivakumar among others. If Belgium pulls off the upset, this group earns 4 points while the vast majority gets nothing.

![ESP v BEL prediction split]({{ site.baseurl }}/active-contest/data/plots/st421/st421_ESP_v_BEL.png){: .img-fluid }

### ENG v NOR — The Match That Splits the Contest in Half

**32 picked Norway. 30 picked England.** 52% vs 48%. This is the most contested prediction in the entire ST-421 round — and arguably the most divisive single match pick across the whole tournament.

All three AI models picked England. The majority of humans disagreed.

![ENG v NOR prediction split]({{ site.baseurl }}/active-contest/data/plots/st421/st421_ENG_v_NOR.png){: .img-fluid }

Among the Norway believers: **Tees** (current overall leader), Prasad R, Sri Iyer, Sanjeev, Shriya Sateesh, Subha, Subbu, Sackett, Vivek Shenoy, Rishi Baveja, and 22 others. Among the England camp: all 3 AIs, Ranga Setlur, Daniel, Ram N, Ranga, Rupal, Rupender, Gokul, and 21 others.

Whoever calls this one correctly earns 4 points. Whoever doesn't, loses ground.

## Who Wins It All?

France are the heavy favourite — 37 of 62 participants (60%) picked them as champions. But the remaining 40% is spread across five different countries, which reflects genuine uncertainty about who meets France in the final.

![ST-421 winner picks]({{ site.baseurl }}/active-contest/data/plots/st421/st421_winner_picks.png){: .img-fluid }

**Norway as champion** is picked by 7 people — Ashish Naik, Chayan, Radhika Santhanam, Sahana, Sanjeev, Shriya Sateesh, and Vishal Goyal. For Norway to win, they'd need to beat England in the QF, advance through the semis, and beat France in the final. That's three upsets in a row. If it happens, those seven will be sitting very pretty.

**Argentina** at 13% reflects the contingent who see a France vs Argentina final going to La Albiceleste — including ChatGPT and Gemini.

**Spain** pickers (5) are betting on a Spain-led bracket, needing Spain to beat Belgium and then advance through the final. **England** (3) and **Morocco** (2) are the true long shots in the field.

## The Contrarians

**Chayan Chakrabarti** is the most contrarian participant in ST-421 by a wide margin. He picked the minority team in all four QFs: Morocco, Belgium, Norway, and Switzerland. His semi picks follow logically — Belgium and Norway — and his winner pick is Norway. If the tournament goes full chaos mode, Chayan is positioned better than anyone. As of QF1, he's already behind: Morocco lost.

**Ashish Naik** is the other notable contrarian: Morocco, Belgium, Norway, Argentina for the QFs, with Morocco in SF1, Norway in SF2, and Norway as champion. He's also out on QF1, but his Norway conviction is unmatched in the field.

**Prasad R** — fresh off winning the R16 — picked Belgium to beat Spain, which would be the round's next big upset. After correctly calling Norway in the R16, Prasad clearly isn't afraid of backing the underdog.

## How Many Humans Agreed with the AIs on All Four QFs?

**21 of 59 humans** (36%) picked the same four QF winners as all three AIs: France, Spain, England, Argentina. That's a minority — meaning 64% of humans deviated from the AI consensus on at least one match. Almost all of the deviation is on the ENG/NOR match, where the human field leans Norway while the AIs are unanimous on England.

The next few days will tell us whether the machines or the humans read Norway better.
