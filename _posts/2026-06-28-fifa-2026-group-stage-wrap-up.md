## The Uruguay Trap

Let's talk about the elephant in the room. **Uruguay** was picked by 96% of participants to finish in the top 2 of Group H. They were eliminated. Every single AI model picked them. The Wisdom of the Crowd picked them. Virtually everyone picked them.

And they went home.

Only **2 participants** — Daniel and Rahul Santhanam — correctly picked Cabo Verde to qualify from Group H (as a top-2 finisher or third-place pick, respectively). Daniel's call to pick **Cabo Verde as a top-2 qualifier** alongside Spain was one of the boldest correct predictions in this contest's history.

Uruguay's elimination was the single most costly miss across the entire field. If you got Group H wrong, you're in good company — 96% of the crowd is right there with you.

**Gemini** and **Claude** both fell into the middle of the pack. Claude's 20 points were hurt by picking Algeria over Austria in Group J and getting the Norway/Senegal order wrong in Group I — small misses that added up.

### The Wisdom of the Crowd

The aggregate "Wisdom of the Crowd" (WOTC) prediction scored **22 points** — right at the human median. Not bad, but not great. The crowd correctly identified the top 2 in 8 of 12 groups but was let down by the same traps (Uruguay, Korea Republic, Ecuador as top-2) that caught most individuals.

## What Made the Difference

Looking at the top scorers, a few patterns emerge:

**1. Getting Group D right.** The USA was easy — 92% had them. But picking Australia over Turkey as the second qualifier separated the sharp predictors from the conventional ones. Alex, Aarush, and others who dodged Turkey here gained an edge.

**2. Côte d'Ivoire over Ecuador in Group E.** Two-thirds of participants had Ecuador finishing top-2. The top scorers who picked CIV instead (Alex, Aarush, Rahul Santhanam) collected points that most of the field missed.

**3. Third-place picks.** The bonus points for correct third-place calls (Bosnia, Paraguay, Sweden, Senegal, Algeria, Ghana, etc.) created meaningful separation. Alex picked up 6 correct third-place calls — more than almost anyone else.

**4. Avoiding Uruguay.** Almost nobody avoided this trap — but those who picked Cabo Verde or correctly identified Group H as volatile gained a huge edge.
