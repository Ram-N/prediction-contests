The Round of 16 is done. Eight matches, three genuine upsets, and two participants who called them all — almost.

**Prasad R** from Newton and **Tees** from Seattle each finished with **28 out of 32 points** — 7 correct picks out of 8, the best score in the round. Their only shared miss? Picking Mexico over England, the one wrong call they had in common with the vast majority of the field.

What set them apart from the other 65 participants was simple: they both picked Norway, Belgium, and Switzerland.

## The Three Picks That Decided the Round

### Norway over Brazil (~18% pick rate)

This was the round's defining result. Brazil were heavy favourites, and **82% of the field backed them**. All three AI models picked Brazil. The Wisdom of the Crowd picked Brazil. It took genuine conviction — or a very good read on Norway's tournament form — to go the other way.

Twelve participants called it. Only two of them also got BEL and SUI.

### Belgium over USA (~25% pick rate)

Three quarters of the field backed the USA on home continent. Belgium quietly dispatched them. ChatGPT and Claude both picked Belgium correctly here — a point in their favour — while Gemini and the Crowd both went with the States.

![USA v BEL prediction split]({{ site.baseurl }}/active-contest/data/plots/r16/r16_USA_v_BEL.png){: .img-fluid }

### Switzerland over Colombia (~19% pick rate)

The quieter of the three upsets, but equally decisive for the leaderboard. Switzerland's win eliminated nearly 80% of participants from a perfect round. Prasad and Tees both had it. None of the three AIs did.

## Humans vs. AI: Mixed Signals

All three AI models were in contention after the Round of 32. The R16 separated them.

| Model | R16 Score | What they got right | What they missed |
|-------|:---------:|---------------------|-----------------|
| ChatGPT | 24 | MAR, FRA, ENG, ESP, BEL, ARG | NOR, SUI |
| Claude | 24 | MAR, FRA, ENG, ESP, BEL, ARG | NOR, SUI |
| Gemini | 16 | MAR, FRA, ESP, ARG | NOR, ENG, BEL, SUI |

**ChatGPT and Claude** submitted identical predictions — for the second time in this contest — and finished together at 24 points. That's a respectable result: they correctly called Belgium over the USA, which tripped up the majority of the field. But they went with Brazil over Norway, and they picked Colombia over Switzerland. Both misses cost them the round.

**Gemini** had a rough outing. At 16 points (4 from 8), it performed at the level of the Wisdom of the Crowd — the aggregated majority pick — which also scored 16. Gemini not only missed Norway and Switzerland, but also backed Mexico over England and the USA over Belgium. Four misses is a significant gap from the 24 that its AI counterparts scored.

A few observations worth noting:
- No AI model picked any of the three upsets.
- ChatGPT and Claude's identical output is a pattern: same predictions again, same result. Whether this is a sign of convergent training on the same football consensus or something else, the effect is indistinguishable.
- Gemini's four misses align with the least-informed consensus picks (back the bigger name in every match). ChatGPT and Claude showed more nuance by calling Belgium, but not enough to identify Norway or Switzerland.
